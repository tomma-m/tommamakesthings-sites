## This website

This section covers **sidelinehero.tommamakesthings.com**. It is separate from the
app policy above, which describes the Sideline Hero app itself.

**If you use the beta request form**, the email address you type is sent to
Formspree, a third-party form service, and forwarded to us. We use it for one
thing: sending you a TestFlight invitation and occasional messages about the beta.
It is not sold, shared, or used for anything else.

**Nothing else is collected.** This site has no analytics, no advertising, no
tracking pixels, and no cookies. Fonts and every other asset are served from this
domain, so loading a page here makes no request to any other company.

**To have your email removed**, write to the support address on the
[support page](/support) and it will be deleted.
