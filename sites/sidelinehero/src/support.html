Reporting a bug

Four things make a bug fixable instead of a mystery:

  • The app version — Help → scroll to the footer.
  • Your device, and its iOS or Android version.
  • What you were doing when it happened.
  • What you expected instead.

Common questions

Does my team data go anywhere?
No. Everything stays on your phone. The app makes no network requests at all — it works with the phone in airplane mode.

Do I need an account?
No. There is no sign-up, no login, and no password.

Where do I change substitution settings?
Help → App settings, inside the app. Every option is explained there.

How do I get my data off the phone?
Game Summary → Share summary sends a readable recap of one game — handy for a team chat. Game Summary → Export CSV gives you the same game as a spreadsheet file, and a team's Season minutes screen has its own Export CSV for the whole season at once.
