Make a fair basketball rotation chart for your team. Pick your roster size and game format, add your players' names if you like, then print it for the bench.

Every chart comes from Sideline Hero's planner, set to equal playing time, with Players 1 to 5 starting. The times are the scoreboard when each stretch starts. Sub at the next dead ball near that time.

Charts cover 6 to 12 players, halves of 16 or 20 minutes, and quarters of 6, 8, 10 or 12 minutes. Read more about each roster size on the rotation charts page.
