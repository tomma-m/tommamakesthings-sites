# 8 player basketball rotation

Eight players means three on the bench at a time. Everyone plays exactly 20 minutes of a 32-minute game. The chart below is for four 8-minute quarters, made by Sideline Hero's planner set to equal playing time, with Players 1 to 5 starting.

{{chart}}

## How this rotation works

Eight players split a 32-minute game perfectly: 20 minutes each. This plan splits the game into eight 4-minute blocks and subs at every one: at each quarter break and at the 4:00 mark of each quarter. Everyone plays five of the eight blocks.

That makes eight one of the easiest rosters to run. Subs only ever happen at the break or halfway through the quarter, so they're easy to remember.

## Tips for eight players

- One mid-quarter change is easy to remember. Many coaches just tell the players at the start of the quarter who's going in at 4:00.
- Quarter breaks are free subs. Use them, so you don't have to stop play.
- If your league plays a running clock, the mid-quarter change comes up fast. Have the next group ready by the scorer's table early.

## How to read the chart

Each column is a stretch of the game, headed by the quarter and the time on the scoreboard when it starts. A dot means that player is on the court. The last column is each player's total minutes for a 32-minute game.

The times are targets, not orders. Sub at the next dead ball near the time on the chart. Being a few seconds out won't change anyone's minutes much.
