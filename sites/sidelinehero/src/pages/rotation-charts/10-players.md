# 10 player basketball rotation

Ten players means five on the bench at a time. Everyone plays exactly 16 minutes of a 32-minute game. The chart below is for four 8-minute quarters, made by Sideline Hero's planner set to equal playing time, with Players 1 to 5 starting.

{{chart}}

## How this rotation works

Ten players is two full fives, and this plan keeps it simple: everyone plays two full quarters and sits two, for exactly 16 minutes each. All the subs happen at quarter breaks. Players 1 to 5 start, Players 6 to 10 play the second quarter, and the groups mix in the second half so the same five aren't always together.

## Tips for ten players

- If your two fives aren't evenly matched, mix them. Put a stronger and a newer player in each group, so neither five gets run off the court.
- Swap the starting five from game to game. The second group should start sometimes too.
- If someone's late or leaves early, this plan breaks. That's when the app earns its keep, because it re-plans everyone's minutes on the spot.

## How to read the chart

Each column is a stretch of the game, headed by the quarter and the time on the scoreboard when it starts. A dot means that player is on the court. The last column is each player's total minutes for a 32-minute game.

The times are targets, not orders. Sub at the next dead ball near the time on the chart. Being a few seconds out won't change anyone's minutes much.
