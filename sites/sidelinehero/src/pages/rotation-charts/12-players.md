# 12 player basketball rotation

Twelve players means seven on the bench at a time. Everyone plays about 13 to 14 minutes of a 32-minute game. The chart below is for four 8-minute quarters, made by Sideline Hero's planner set to equal playing time, with Players 1 to 5 starting.

{{chart}}

## How this rotation works

Twelve is a big roster. An even split is 13:20 each, and with seven sitting at any time the chart makes three or four changes a quarter, anywhere from one player to a whole five. This plan lands everyone between 12:45 and 14:15.

## Tips for twelve players

- Your biggest job is the bench. Seven players waiting is the most likely to cause frustration, so let them know when they're going in next.
- Fair minutes take a plan. Coaches running twelve without one often find their best five played twice as much as the rest.
- With this many players, a late arrival or an injury upsets the whole chart. The app re-plans everyone's minutes when that happens, so you don't have to.

## How to read the chart

Each column is a stretch of the game, headed by the quarter and the time on the scoreboard when it starts. A dot means that player is on the court. The last column is each player's total minutes for a 32-minute game.

The times are targets, not orders. Sub at the next dead ball near the time on the chart. Being a few seconds out won't change anyone's minutes much.
