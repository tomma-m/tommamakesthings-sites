# 7 player basketball rotation

Seven players means two on the bench at a time. Everyone plays about 22 to 23 minutes of a 32-minute game. The chart below is for four 8-minute quarters, made by Sideline Hero's planner set to equal playing time, with Players 1 to 5 starting.

{{chart}}

## How this rotation works

Two players sit at any time, and the chart swaps one or two at a time, about five changes a quarter. An even split of 32 minutes across seven players is 22:51, and this plan lands everyone between 22:00 and 23:00.

## Tips for seven players

- Seven is a comfortable number. Everyone gets real rest and real minutes.
- Swapping one or two at a time keeps your court settled. You're never changing most of the team at once.
- Keep an eye on fouls. Losing a player takes you to six, where only one can rest at a time.

## How to read the chart

Each column is a stretch of the game, headed by the quarter and the time on the scoreboard when it starts. A dot means that player is on the court. The last column is each player's total minutes for a 32-minute game.

The times are targets, not orders. Sub at the next dead ball near the time on the chart. Being a few seconds out won't change anyone's minutes much.
