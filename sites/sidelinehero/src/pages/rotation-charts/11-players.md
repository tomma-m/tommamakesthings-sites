# 11 player basketball rotation

Eleven players means six on the bench at a time. Everyone plays about 14 to 15 minutes of a 32-minute game. The chart below is for four 8-minute quarters, made by Sideline Hero's planner set to equal playing time, with Players 1 to 5 starting.

{{chart}}

## How this rotation works

With eleven, more than half your team is on the bench at any moment. An even split is 14:33 each. This plan changes a few players at a time, about four times a quarter, and lands everyone between 14:00 and 15:30.

## Tips for eleven players

- Keep the bench involved. Six kids sitting is a lot. Give them a job, like watching a matchup or counting the other team's fouls.
- Short stints are normal here. Some players get on for under a minute between changes, which is fine for young players.
- If your league allows it, longer quarters help. The same chart with 10-minute quarters gives everyone more time on the court each stint.

## How to read the chart

Each column is a stretch of the game, headed by the quarter and the time on the scoreboard when it starts. A dot means that player is on the court. The last column is each player's total minutes for a 32-minute game.

The times are targets, not orders. Sub at the next dead ball near the time on the chart. Being a few seconds out won't change anyone's minutes much.
