# 6 player basketball rotation

Six players means one on the bench at a time. Everyone plays about 26 to 27 minutes of a 32-minute game. The chart below is for four 8-minute quarters, made by Sideline Hero's planner set to equal playing time, with Players 1 to 5 starting.

{{chart}}

## How this rotation works

With six players there's only ever one kid sitting, so the rotation is really a rest schedule. Each player takes the bench spot four times a game, for short rests of a minute or two. Nobody plays the whole game, and nobody sits for long.

An even split of 32 minutes across six players is 26:40 each. This plan lands everyone between 26:00 and 27:00.

## Tips for six players

- Fouls matter more. If one player fouls out you're down to five with nobody to rest, so watch anyone on three or four fouls and give them their rest early.
- Short rests work better than long ones. Two minutes on the bench is enough to get their breath back without losing focus.
- Tell the player coming off when they'll be back on. It stops the bench spot feeling like a punishment.

## How to read the chart

Each column is a stretch of the game, headed by the quarter and the time on the scoreboard when it starts. A dot means that player is on the court. The last column is each player's total minutes for a 32-minute game.

The times are targets, not orders. Sub at the next dead ball near the time on the chart. Being a few seconds out won't change anyone's minutes much.
