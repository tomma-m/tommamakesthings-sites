# Coaching guides

Short, practical guides for youth and rec basketball coaches. They're about the part of game day nobody trains you for: who plays, when, and for how long.

- [How to sub fairly in youth basketball](/guides/fair-subs-youth-basketball). The whole approach, from planning before tip-off to the last sub of the game.
- [Equal playing time in rec league basketball](/guides/equal-playing-time). What "equal" actually means, why it's hard on game day, and how to talk to parents about it.
- [Running a rotation with 10 or more players](/guides/big-roster-rotation). Two fives, quarter breaks, and keeping a big bench happy.

Looking for a chart to print? There's one for every roster size from 6 to 12 on the [rotation charts page](/rotation-charts).
