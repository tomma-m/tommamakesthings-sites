# Basketball rotation charts

Free rotation charts for youth basketball, planned for equal playing time. Pick your roster size, print the chart and take it to the game.

Every chart here comes from Sideline Hero's planner, the same one the app uses before tip-off. Each player gets their fair share of the game, within about a minute and a half of everyone else.

## Pick your roster size

- [6 players](/rotation-charts/6-players): one on the bench at a time, about 26 to 27 minutes each
- [7 players](/rotation-charts/7-players): two on the bench, about 22 to 23 minutes each
- [8 players](/rotation-charts/8-players): three on the bench, exactly 20 minutes each
- [9 players](/rotation-charts/9-players): four on the bench, about 17 to 18 minutes each
- [10 players](/rotation-charts/10-players): two full fives, exactly 16 minutes each
- [11 players](/rotation-charts/11-players): six on the bench, about 14 to 15 minutes each
- [12 players](/rotation-charts/12-players): seven on the bench, about 13 to 14 minutes each

Minutes are for four 8-minute quarters. For halves, or quarters of 6, 10 or 12 minutes, and to put your players' names on the chart, use the [rotation chart maker](/rotation-chart-maker).

## What a chart can't do

A chart assumes the game goes to plan. Real games don't. A kid fouls out, someone rolls an ankle, a player turns up at halftime. Then the chart is wrong for everyone, and working out new minutes from the bench while the clock runs is hard.
