You're on the sideline. The timer is running. Someone needs to come off — but who's been on the longest? Who hasn't had their fair share of court time?

Most coaches solve this by scribbling on a clipboard, losing track halfway through the second quarter, and spending the rest of the game with a nagging feeling that someone's getting short-changed on minutes.

Plan before the game starts

Tell Sideline Hero your starting five and how you want rotations to work. It builds a full substitution plan, balancing playing time across the whole roster. You choose the priority — equal time for everyone, more minutes for developing players, or your strongest lineup when it counts.

Follow along live

The game clock ticks alongside the action. When a substitution window approaches, the app alerts you — before the moment, not after. Planned subs are queued and ready in two taps. Need an unplanned change? Smart Pick suggests who needs court time most.

Track stats without a statistician

Tap points, fouls and opponent scores as they happen. Court time is logged to the second, so you never have to wonder who played how long.

See the season, not just the game

Every game adds to a running record. Browse your history to see how the season is going, or open any player to track their development over time.

Built for youth basketball

Designed for coaches running youth and community programs, where fair playing time isn't just good coaching — it's an obligation to every kid and their family. Rosters of 5 to 12 players, flexible game formats.
