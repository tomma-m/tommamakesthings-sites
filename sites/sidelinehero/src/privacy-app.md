# Privacy Policy

**Sideline Hero**
Effective date: 2026-04-01
Last updated: 2026-08-02

## Overview

Sideline Hero is a basketball substitution and stats tracking app for coaches. This policy explains what information the app handles and how it is stored and used.

The short version: all your data stays on your device. Sideline Hero does not transmit any information to any server, does not have user accounts, and does not collect analytics. Your team data is yours.

## Information We Collect

Sideline Hero stores only the information you enter directly into the app. This includes:

- **Team information** — team name and colour preferences
- **Coach name** — optional, used only to personalise the app's greeting
- **Team logo** — an image you choose from your photo library, copied into the app's own storage. The app has no other access to your photos.
- **Player information** — player names and jersey numbers, skill level (optional), and active/inactive status, including the names of one-off fill-in players added to a single game
- **Game records** — opponent name, date, location, game configuration settings, substitution history, and in-game events
- **Game statistics** — points scored, fouls, playing time, and season totals calculated from completed games

Sideline Hero does not collect any information about you as the user. There are no user accounts, no login, and no device identifiers linked to your data.

## How We Use Your Information

The information you enter is used solely to operate the features of the app:

- Player and team data is used to plan substitution rotations and track playing time
- Game statistics are accumulated into season totals to help you monitor player development
- Recent opponent names and locations are remembered to speed up game setup via autocomplete

Your data is never used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than operating the features you see in the app.

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally on your device using iOS and Android standard local storage (AsyncStorage). No data is transmitted to any external server at any time. There is no cloud backup, no sync, and no remote storage of any kind in this version of the app.

Because data is stored only on your device:

- Deleting the app will permanently delete all your data
- Data is not transferred if you change devices unless you use a device backup service (such as iCloud or Google Drive backup) that includes app data

You can delete all data stored by Sideline Hero at any time by deleting the app from your device, which permanently removes everything it has stored.

## Data Sharing

Sideline Hero does not share your data with anyone. Specifically:

- No data is sent to Sideline Hero or any company associated with it
- No data is shared with third parties, advertisers, or analytics providers
- No third-party SDKs that collect or transmit data are included in this version of the app
- There is no advertising of any kind in the app

The app does include a share/export feature, which you operate yourself: if you use it, the game data you export goes wherever you choose to send it, using your device's own sharing options. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you do that.

## Children's Privacy

Sideline Hero is designed for use by coaches and is not directed at children. Player profiles within the app may contain names and jersey numbers of young athletes; however, this information is stored only on the coach's device, is never transmitted by the app, and leaves your device only if you choose to share it.

If you are a parent or guardian and have concerns about information entered into the app, please contact the coach who manages the team, as they control the device and data. You may also contact us directly using the details below.

Sideline Hero does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

## No Analytics or Crash Reporting

This version of Sideline Hero does not include any analytics, usage tracking, or crash reporting tools. We do not receive any information about how the app is used or whether it has experienced errors on your device.

## No In-App Purchases or Payment Processing

This version of Sideline Hero does not include in-app purchases, subscriptions, or any payment processing. No financial information of any kind is collected.

## Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes in a future version of the app, the updated policy will be published in the app and on the app listing page. The "Last updated" date at the top of this document will reflect when changes were made.
